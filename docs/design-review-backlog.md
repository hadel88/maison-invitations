# Design review decisions

## Ivory Garden — current review
- Preserve the raised embroidered paper character.
- Keep the original 5.5-second opening swing, original easing and ±112-degree paper rotation. Do not replace the approved motion.
- The rounded inner page should be subtly translucent.
- Calendar/location buttons must fit inside the paper card; RSVP controls must match its stationery style.
- Replace the flat name rectangle with a dimensional personalised plaque.
- The plaque stays attached to the right paper panel and opens with it. It must not detach or fall.
- Give every design its own plaque silhouette and decorative treatment, matching its occasion and motif.
- Carry the visual theme through the opening, inner invitation, event information and RSVP.
- Cover all ten existing occasions. Include boys, girls and neutral designs for birthdays, baby showers, christenings and graduations.
